Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Lixr is operated by The Cruzn Lounge LLC (“we”). Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

What stays on your device

Your saved recipes, recipes you write, favorites, collections, My Bar ingredients, shopping list, recent clones and settings are stored only in your browser on your device. We don’t have accounts and we can’t see this data. You can delete it any time in Profile → Clear my data.

Photos and descriptions you send to clone a drink

When you scan a menu, take a photo of a drink or describe a drink, that photo or text is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to our AI provider, Anthropic, to create the recipe. We don’t store your photos or descriptions on our server. Anthropic processes them under its commercial API terms, which by default don’t allow API data to be used to train its models. The camera is only used while the Scan or Photo screen is open.

Reports

If you tap “Report this result”, we receive the drink name, the recipe, the reason you chose and any note you write. We use reports only to improve Lixr’s results.

Server logs and limits

To stop abuse, our server keeps short-lived counters of requests per network address. These are deleted within about two hours. Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Lixr has no ads and no third-party analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

Alcohol and age

Lixr includes recipes with alcohol, intended for people of legal drinking age (21+ in the US). People under 21 can use mocktails-only mode. Lixr is not directed to children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
